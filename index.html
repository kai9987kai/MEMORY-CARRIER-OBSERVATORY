Removal effects beyond additivity

A term for subset S is the alternating sum of accuracy for S and every subset of S. Pair terms subtract the two single-removal effects; higher orders also subtract all lower-order terms. Positive values mean greater accuracy than that additive decomposition predicts.

THE QUESTION

Where is a learned trace carried?

A cohort learns a binary cue → outcome mapping, then faces a transfer context. The reference arm keeps four channels. The experimental arm loses one selected channel after training. Both arms share each seed's mapping, synthetic training traces, and test-noise draws.

01Internal policyA trace stored within the individual agent.
02Developed body patternA trace stored in a synthetic morphological state.
03Habitat imprintAn external cue mark that remains in a familiar world.
04Colony relayA shared signal available to experienced group members.
DESIGN & SCORING

Paired by construction

  • Every seed pair shares one hidden cue rule, training draw, transfer context, subject cohort, and decision-noise stream.
  • The two arms differ only by the selected post-training carrier removal, plus any transfer change applied identically to both.
  • Scores are correct choices divided by test choices. The reported interval is a normal-approximation interval over seed-level paired differences.
  • The probe planner ranks candidate removals by disagreement across five fixed, illustrative carrier-storage hypotheses.
Interpretation boundary

The channel reliabilities, weights, agents, and traces are hand-authored toy parameters. They do not estimate a biological quantity. Randomized noise is deterministic for a given protocol and seed.

REPRODUCIBILITY

Protocol fingerprint

Each run records its settings, app version, seed-pair receipts, carrier intervention, transfer context, generated timestamp, and a deterministic run key. Export JSON for the complete run, or CSV for the paired seed rows.

Storage

PROJECT LINEAGE

A synthesis, not a claim of priority

The Memory Carrier Observatory draws design ideas from the linked experiments and tools: seeded simulation, matched interventions, model auditing, searchable evidence, specialist systems, local-first workspaces, and real-world verification practices. No source code or model weights are copied into this prototype.

A quick public search found adjacent memory-transfer benchmarks and artificial-life projects. This build makes no claim to be the first project of its kind; its distinctive focus is a unified carrier-knockout × transfer-context experiment with paired receipts and a disagreement-guided next probe.
